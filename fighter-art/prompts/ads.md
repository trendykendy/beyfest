# Beyfest 2026 fake ads: prompts

Still images with a voiceover, 10 to 15 seconds each, for downtime at the
tournament. Same workflow as the intro: ChatGPT in the built-in browser, the
user attaches, Claude types, one chat per image.

Any text on screen (captions, end cards) is added in code, so it stays sharp.
The images themselves have none. The titles copy American local TV: chunky gold
and yellow lettering with black outlines and hard shadows, blue and red bands,
sunburst rays, a starburst for the slogan.

## Shared style

> Anime key-visual illustration, matching the hand-drawn rendering of the
> attached references exactly: confident inked line work whose weight varies
> like a pen, thicker on outer contours; crisp cel-shadow shapes; sharp-edged
> highlights. Fine hand-drawn detail, but no heavy comic-book cross-hatching, no
> airbrushed gradients, no soft blur, no photorealism. Landscape 3:2. The image
> will be cropped to 16:9, so keep everything important away from the top and
> bottom edges. No text, letters, numbers, logos or emblems anywhere.

The first try looked like a real actor, so the head was replaced in the same
chat ("does not resemble any real person or celebrity": rounder face, flat
eyebrows, limp comb-over) and a cheap, crooked framed picture of a battling top
was added on the wall. It sits high, so the 16:9 crop for A2 is rows 60 to 924,
and the lower third covers where that clips his hand.
