# The Fairy Dancer: likeness sheet

Player: Steve. From 2 reference photos (in the git-ignored
`reference-photos/fairydancer/`), 2026-10-31. Upload copies, cropped so only he is in
them, are in `reference-photos/fairydancer/upload/`.

**The master reference is the beach selfie** (`2-photo-master-front.jpg`): it's the
only one without a cap, and the cap is gone in the redesign (user's call), so it's
the one that shows his hair. The laughing photo (`3-photo-laughing.jpg`, in a navy
cap) is for the laugh, the eyes and the cheeks.

## Anchors (carry these into every image)

- **Hair:** light brown, cropped very short at the sides; a high, receding hairline
  with thin hair on top, so a big, high forehead. Not full, not spiky, not swept.
- **Face shape:** a long oval, with a long chin and a slightly rounded jaw; full,
  rounded cheeks that bunch up high when he laughs. Not square or chiselled.
- **Facial hair:** light stubble only, sandy to ginger along the jaw and chin. No
  beard.
- **Eyes:** blue, fairly small and deep-set; they squint and crinkle almost shut
  when he laughs. Light, fairly straight brows that knit a little in the middle.
- **Nose:** fairly large and straight, with a rounded tip.
- **Ears:** noticeable, sticking out a little.
- **Mouth:** a wide, open, toothy laugh with the top teeth showing.
- **Skin:** fair, flushing pink at the cheeks and nose.
- **Build:** slim to average, not muscular.

## Where the current art has drifted

The current `fairydancer.png` (in the app) has:
- a Nike cap hiding the hairline, where the redesign shows his real hair
- a tanned, square-jawed, chiselled hero with a dark goatee, where Steve is fair,
  long-faced and round-cheeked with only light stubble
- brown eyes, where his are blue
- a fist-forward lunge too close to the Pugilist's pose

## Exaggerate slightly

The high forehead, the round cheeks bunching up, the eyes crinkled shut and the
big open laugh.
